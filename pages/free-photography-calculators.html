Why is it free? (the honest answer)

ShutterMath is a small independent project. The calculators cost nothing to run (they live in your browser), and the site is funded only by optional, clearly-labeled ads — which you can decline without losing a single feature. No paywalls, no premium tiers, no selling your data, no "free trial" tricks. Free means free.

Free calculator FAQ

Which calculators are free?

All of them — the entire site. Depth of field, hyperfocal, ND filter, exposure, flash guide number, star trails, pixel scale, field of view, shutter angle, video ND, macro DoF, and print resolution. Plus the reference charts.

Is there a pro or paid version?

No. There is exactly one version, and it's free. If you see a site charging for these tools, they're charging for math that's been public domain since the 1800s.

Can I use the tools for commercial work?

Yes — use them for client shoots, print sales, anything. Your results are yours. See the terms for the fine print.
