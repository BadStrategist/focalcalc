Frequently asked questions

Are these calculators free?

Yes — every tool on FocalCalc is free, runs entirely in your browser, and works offline once loaded. No accounts, no watermarks, no limits.

Why do results differ from other calculators?

The two usual culprits: the circle-of-confusion standard (we use the common diagonal÷1500 convention, applied from your camera's exact sensor size) and whether the formula adds the focal-length term. Our formulas are the standard published ones.

Can I print the results?

Yes. Every calculator page has a print button that produces a clean cheat sheet — just the results and the formula reference, no navigation or controls.

Do you store my data?

No. Your setups are saved only in your own browser's local storage, and we use privacy-respecting analytics to understand which tools help people. See our Privacy Policy.
